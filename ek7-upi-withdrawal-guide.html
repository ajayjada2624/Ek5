EK5 UPI Withdrawal Guide

Learn how to securely and instantly withdraw your game winnings from the EK5 App straight to your bank account using UPI or IMPS.

Withdrawal Limits & Speed Overview

Transaction Mode Minimum Limit Maximum Limit Processing Time

How to Withdraw Money from EK5 App

  1. Open the App: Launch the EK5 App on your Android device and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Wallet: Tap on the Withdraw or Wallet section on the main dashboard.
  3. Add Payment Details: Link your verified UPI ID (or bank account details for IMPS transfers). Make sure the name matches your profile KYC.
  4. Enter Amount: Type in the amount you want to withdraw (Minimum limit is ₹100).
  5. Submit Request: Tap confirm. Your payout will be processed and credited to your account within 5 to 30 minutes.

Important Withdrawal Rules

  • Ensure your mobile number and KYC details are correctly verified before initiating a payout.
  • Bonus balances or unplayed deposit amounts may have wagering requirements before they become withdrawable winnings.
  • In case of any delays, you can contact support directly via the in-house help desk or email us at support@ek5.co.in.
