How to Withdraw Money from EK5 App

  1. Open the App: Launch the EK5 App on your Android device and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Wallet: Tap on the Withdraw or Wallet section on the main dashboard.
  3. Add Payment Details: Link your verified UPI ID (or bank account details for IMPS transfers). Make sure the name matches your profile details.
  4. Enter Amount: Type in the amount you want to withdraw (minimum limit is ₹100 for UPI and ₹500 for IMPS).
  5. Submit Request: Tap confirm and wait for the payout to process rather than submitting repeated requests while one is pending.

Important Withdrawal Rules

  • Ensure your mobile number and profile/KYC details are accurately verified before initiating a payout.
  • Bonus balances or unplayed deposit amounts may have wagering or turnover requirements before becoming withdrawable.
  • Double-check your UPI ID or IFSC/account number so transfers do not fail or get delayed.
  • If a payout is delayed, check your wallet transaction log first, then contact support via the in-app help desk or email support@ek5.co.in.

Quick Withdrawal Questions

What is the minimum withdrawal amount for UPI and IMPS?

The listed minimum is ₹100 for UPI (up to ₹25,000/day, typically 5 to 30 minutes) and ₹500 for IMPS bank transfer (up to ₹50,000/day, typically 30 minutes to 2 hours).

Why might a withdrawal request be held or delayed?

Common reasons include account holder name mismatches, wrong UPI or bank details, incomplete turnover/wagering conditions, multiple simultaneous requests, or bank/gateway downtime.
